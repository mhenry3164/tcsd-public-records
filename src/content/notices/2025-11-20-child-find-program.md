Child Find Program for Tishomingo County School District

The Tishomingo County School District is participating in an ongoing statewide effort to identify, locate, and evaluate children birth through the age of twenty-one who may have a physical, mental, communicative, and/or emotional disability. Early identification of children in need of special education services is critical to their future success.

Furthermore, information that is gathered from contacts with parents and other stakeholders will be used to help determine present and future program needs as progress is made toward the goal of providing a free appropriate public education to all disabled children. You can help by referring any student from birth through the age of twenty-one with any of the above disabling conditions to Mr. Cliff Nunley, Child Find Contact Person for the district, at 662-423-3206. You may also stop by TCSD Central Office at 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka MS. Individuals and stakeholders making referrals may remain anonymous if they desire.
