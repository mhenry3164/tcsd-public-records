ADVERTISEMENT FOR PROPOSALS

Notice is hereby given that the Tishomingo County School District will receive sealed bid proposals for

E-Rate Category 2 Firewall Project

Bids must be received no later than December 16, 2025 at 10:00 AM CST. Vendors may submit proposals in one of the following ways:

Email Submission:  
kcounce@tcsk12.com with subject line: E-Rate Category 2 Firewall Project

Mailed/Delivered Sealed Bids:  
Tishomingo County School District  
E-Rate Category 2 Firewall Project  
1620 Paul Edmondson Drive  
Iuka, MS 38852

All required documents, including the Specification Response Form and vendor materials, must be included at the time of submission.

An Optional Site Visit is available on December 2 at 10:00 AM CST at 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS 38852.

The Tishomingo County School District is an equal opportunity employer and hereby notifies all bidders that it will affirmatively insure that, in any contract entered unto pursuant to this advertisement, minority business enterprises will be afforded full opportunity to submit bids in response to this invitation and will not be discriminated against on the grounds of race, color, sex, age, disability or national origin in consideration for an award.

The Tishomingo County School District reserves the right to reject any and or all bids, waive technicalities, informalities or irregularities in the bids received, solicit new bids or to choose that bid which is deemed to be in the best interest of the Tishomingo County School District.

Jessica Seaton, Board President  
Tishomingo County School District Board of Education
