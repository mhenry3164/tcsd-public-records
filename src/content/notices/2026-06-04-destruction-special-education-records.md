PUBLIC NOTICE

Destruction of Special Education Records for students who attended Tishomingo County Schools.

If you were born on or before September 1, 1998 you have until June 30, 2026 to request copies of your records.

Records may be retrieved from: Tishomingo County School District, 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, Mississippi 38852.

If you have questions contact: Mr. Cliff Nunley, Director of Special Education Tishomingo County School District, Phone-662-423-3206.
