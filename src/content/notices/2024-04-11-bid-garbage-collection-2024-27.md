Legal Notice

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Trustees, Iuka, Mississippi, will receive sealed bids for Garbage Collection services for the fiscal years beginning July 1, 2024 through June 30, 2027.

Bids will be received at the Superintendent’s Office, 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS 38852 no later than Tuesday, May 7, 2024 at 1:00 P.M.

Information may be obtained from the Superintendent’s Office or by phone at 662-423-3206.

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Education reserves the right to reject any or all bids.

TISHOMINGO COUNTY SPECIAL MUNICIPAL SEPARATE SCHOOL DISTRICT BOARD OF TRUSTEES  
Jeff Daniel, President
