Legal Notice

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Trustees, Iuka, Mississippi, will receive sealed bids for 2024-2025 SY Gasoline/Diesel Fuel.

Bids will be received at the Superintendent’s Office, 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS 38852 no later than Tuesday, May 7, 2024 at 2:00 P.M.

Bid form may be obtained from the Superintendent’s Office at 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS 38852, or by email by contacting mrobinson@tcsk12.com, or by fax at 662-424-9820.

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Education reserves the right to reject any or all bids.

TISHOMINGO COUNTY SPECIAL MUNICIPAL SEPARATE SCHOOL DISTRICT BOARD OF TRUSTEES  
Jeff Daniel, President
