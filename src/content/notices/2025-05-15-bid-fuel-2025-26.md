LEGAL NOTICE

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Trustees, Iuka, Mississippi, will receive sealed bids for 2025-2026 SY Gasoline/Diesel Fuel.

Bids will be received at the Superintendent’s Office, 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS 38852 no later than Tuesday, June 3, 2025 at 2:00 P.M.

Bid form may be obtained from the Superintendent’s Office at 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS 38852, or by email by contacting mrobinson@tcsk12.com, or by fax at 662-424-9820.

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Education reserves the right to reject any or all bids.

TISHOMINGO COUNTY SPECIAL MUNICIPAL SEPARATE SCHOOL DISTRICT
