LEGAL NOTICE

Sale of Vehicles

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Trustees, Iuka, Mississippi, will receive sealed bids for the sale of the following vehicles:

- Vehicle 1 2003 Ford Escape TCSD Asset # 9945
- Vehicle 2 2010 Ford Escape TCSD Asset # 99955
- Vehicle 3 1995 Ford Van CS3 TCSD Asset # 1238

Bids will be received at the Superintendent’s Office, 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS 38852 no later than Friday, November 8, 2024, 10:00 A.M.

To view the vehicles or for more information, call Belmont High School’s Office 662-454-7924.

The sealed bid should be clearly marked “Vehicle #1, #2, #3”. Bidder must include name, address, phone number, and amount of bid, and signature on the bid. All bids must be hand delivered or mailed to Superintendent’s Office, 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS 38852.

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Education reserves the right to reject any or all bids.

TISHOMINGO COUNTY SPECIAL MUNICIPAL SEPARATE SCHOOL DISTRICT  
BOARD OF TRUSTEES  
Jeff Daniel, President
