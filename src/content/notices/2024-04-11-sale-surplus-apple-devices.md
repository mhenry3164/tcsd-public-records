Legal Notice

Sale of Surplus Computer Equipment – Apple Devices

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Trustees, Iuka, Mississippi, will receive sealed and/or electronic bids for the sale of the following:

- iPad 5th Generation
- iPad Pro 9.7”
- iPad 6th Generation
- iPad Pro 10.5”
- iPad 7th Generation
- iPad Pro 11”
- iPad 8th Generation
- 7th/8th Generation Cases

Bids will be received at the Superintendent’s Office, 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS 38852 no later than Thursday, May 9, 2024, 1:00 P.M.

Specifications are on file in the office of the Superintendent at 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive in Iuka, Mississippi. All bid information may be obtained via the following web address: www.tcsdbids.com. You may also obtain the documents in person at the Superintendent Office of the Tishomingo County School District, located at 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, Mississippi.

To view the devices or for more information, contact Kristy Counce, Technology Director at 662-438-8159 or email: kcounce@tcsk12.com.

Purchases will be awarded to the highest and best bidder, except the Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Education reserves the right to reject any or all bids. Bids shall not be withdrawn prior to the end of the bid period. The sealed bid should be clearly marked “BID – Sale of Surplus Computer Equipment”. Bidder must include name, address, phone number, and amount of bid, and signature on the bid.

TISHOMINGO COUNTY SPECIAL MUNICIPAL SEPARATE SCHOOL DISTRICT BOARD OF TRUSTEES  
Jeff Daniel, President
