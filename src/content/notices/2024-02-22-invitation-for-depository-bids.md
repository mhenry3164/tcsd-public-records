Tishomingo County School District  
1620 Paul Edmondson Drive  
Iuka, MS 38852

Invitation for Depository Bids

The Board of Trustees of the Tishomingo County School District will receive sealed bids until 1:00 p.m., Thursday, March 21, 2024, in the District Administration Office located at 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS, for the privilege of serving as a depository for the funds of the Tishomingo County School District July 1, 2024 through June 30, 2027.

Proposals must be submitted on the Depository Bid Form. Any bid not submitted on the provided bid form will be rejected. Instructions and bid forms can be obtained from the office of the Director of Finance, Tishomingo County School District Administration Office, 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS.

The bid must conform to Section #37-7-333 of the Mississippi Code.

The Board reserves the right to reject any and all bids.

Done by authority of the Board of Trustees on this 15th day of February, 2024.

Jeff Daniel, President
