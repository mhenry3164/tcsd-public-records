Legal Notice

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Trustees, Iuka, Mississippi, will conduct public auctions for the sale of 25 Sets of JBL Professional Control 2P Speakers (set includes 1 Master Speaker and 1 Extension Speaker), 15 Document Cameras used in classrooms and 4 Cisco Access Points. The public auction will take place on www.GovDeals.com/tcsd beginning June 3, 2024 at 7:00 am CST and ending June 17, 2024 at 2:00 pm CST.

Information may be obtained from the Technology Office via email kcounce@tcsk12.com.

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District Board of Education reserves the right to reject any or all bids.

TISHOMINGO COUNTY SPECIAL MUNICIPAL SEPARATE SCHOOL DISTRICT  
BOARD OF TRUSTEES
