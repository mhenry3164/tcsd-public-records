PUBLIC NOTICE

Public Meeting for Tishomingo County Federal Education Grant Program Applications

The Tishomingo County Special Municipal Separate School District is preparing for its 2026-2027 Annual Programs (ESSA: Title I, Title II, and Title IV) and Individuals with Disabilities in Education (IDEA) Budget Applications. A public meeting will be held on Thursday, June 18, 2026, at 9:00 am, at the Tishomingo Elementary School Auditorium, located at 32A East Monroe Street, Tishomingo, MS.

The purpose of this meeting is to gather input and discuss the components of the budget applications. All interested parties are invited to attend. For more information, contact Mr. Cliff Nunley at 662-423-3206.
