ADVERTISEMENT FOR PROPOSALS

Notice is hereby given that the Tishomingo County School District will receive bid proposals for

2025 SOUND SYSTEM SCHOOL GYM AND AUDITORIUM PROJECT

The deadline for receipt of proposals is July 1, 2025, at 2:00 PM CT. Proposals, including Specification Response Form and all other documents, shall be submitted electronically at www.tcsdbids.com.

This bid will be procured through a multi-step procurement process, including a Reverse Auction. Interested bidders will submit bid responses and technical proposals in order to participate in the electronic reverse auction. Pricing will only be accepted through the reverse auction process per the instructions in the bid package. Tishomingo County School District encourages vendor participation in this multi-step process. Complete instructions are provided in the bid package.

A Mandatory Meeting/WaIk through is required on June 26, 2025, at 9:00 AM CT at 1620 Paul Edmondson Drive, Iuka, MS 38852. Parts list will be provided to all participants at the meeting.

Specifications and detailed instructions regarding the bid process may be obtained by visiting the website www.tcsdbids.com. Bidding will be held via an electronic reverse auction process on July 8, 2025, at 2:00 PM CT, as per the detailed bid instructions. For any questions relating to the electronic submittal or reverse auction process, please call PH Bidding Group at 662-407-0193 or email bids@phbidding.com.

The Tishomingo County School District is an equal opportunity employer and hereby notifies all bidders that it will affirmatively insure that, in any contract entered into pursuant to this advertisement, minority business enterprises will be afforded full opportunity to submit bids in response to this invitation and will not be discriminated against on the grounds of race, color, sex, age, disability or national origin in consideration for an award.

The Tishomingo County School District reserves the right to reject any and or all bids, waive technicalities, informalities or irregularities in the bids received, solicit new bids or to choose that bid which is deemed to be in the best interest of the Tishomingo County School District.

Jessica Seaton, Board President  
Tishomingo County School District Board of Education
