MILLAGE RATE NOTICE FOR TISHOMINGO COUNTY SPECIAL MUNICIPAL SEPARATE SCHOOL DISTRICT

A millage rate of 46.50 will produce the same amount of revenue from ad valorem taxes for Tishomingo County School System as was collected the prior year. The millage rate for the previous year was 51 mills.
